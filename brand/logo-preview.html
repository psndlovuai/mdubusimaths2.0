Design notes

The gold bar above the M references mathematical overline notation — M̄ denotes the mean of M. It's a quiet, literate nod to the domain rather than a literal math prop. The green dot anchors the composition and carries the palette's third colour without making the mark feel busy. The warm-white M (not pure white) softens contrast and feels editorial rather than tech-brand.
