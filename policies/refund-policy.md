## 1. Scope

This policy explains when we refund payments. It forms part of our [Terms of Service](./terms-of-service.md) and operates alongside our [Cancellation Policy](./cancellation-policy.md).

## 2. When refunds are available

We refund your payment — in full or in part — in the following circumstances:

### 2.1 You cancel before the cut-off

Refunds for Student-initiated cancellations are governed by the [Cancellation Policy](./cancellation-policy.md) § 2. In summary:

- Cancelled **24 hours or more** before start → full refund.
- Cancelled **less than 24 hours** before start → no refund (reschedule may be offered).

### 2.2 We cancel the session

If we cancel a session for any reason, you receive a full refund on request, or a free reschedule. See [Cancellation Policy](./cancellation-policy.md) § 4.

### 2.3 Technical failure on our side

If a session cannot take place because of a verified fault on our side — for example, our tutor's internet outage, a platform bug that prevented access, or our video link failing to connect — you receive either:

- A full refund of the session fee, or
- A free reschedule of equivalent duration.

A technical failure on **your** side (your internet, your device, your power) is not grounds for a refund. We may offer a goodwill reschedule at our discretion.

### 2.4 Duplicate or erroneous charges

If you are charged twice for the same session, or charged in error, email [support@mdubusistats.com] with the transaction reference. We will verify with Paystack and refund within **7 business days** of confirmation.

### 2.5 Statutory cooling-off (ECT Act section 44)

Where section 44 of the ECT Act applies — principally, where you have entered into an electronic transaction for goods or services and the service has **not yet begun** — you have a right to cancel within **7 days** of payment without reason or penalty, and to receive a full refund. A cancellation under this clause:

- Must be made by email to [support@mdubusistats.com] or via your dashboard;
- Does **not** apply once the session has already started or been delivered;
- Does **not** apply to group sessions once other students have booked in reliance on your booking (in which case your cancellation is handled under the Cancellation Policy).

## 3. How refunds are issued

- Refunds are paid back to the original payment method used through Paystack.
- We initiate the refund within **3 business days** of confirming you are eligible.
- Paystack typically credits the card within **5 to 10 business days**, depending on your issuing bank. We cannot shorten that window.
- Refunds are made in South African Rand (ZAR). If your card is in another currency, the exchange rate applied by your issuing bank may differ slightly from the rate at the time of the original charge; we cannot refund the difference.

---

## 4. Subscription refunds

On the **Monthly** plan (R1,500 per month, up to 24 hours):

- Refunds for the current month are not automatic. We issue a pro-rated refund for **unused hours** only if the reason for termination is on our side (our cancellation, our technical failure, or a breach of these terms by us).
- A Student-initiated cancellation prevents future renewals but does not refund the current month.

### 4.1 How pro-rata is calculated

When a pro-rata refund is due on the Monthly plan, the refundable amount is:

```
refund = (R1,500 ÷ 24 hours) × unused hours
       = R62.50 × unused hours
```

"Unused hours" means the allowance of 24 hours for the month, less any hours already scheduled, attended, or forfeited through late cancellation within that month. The calculation is rounded to the nearest rand in your favour.

## 5. Non-refundable items

The following are not refundable (except where law requires):

- Sessions already delivered.
- Sessions missed by the Student without notice (no-shows).
- Sessions cancelled within **2 hours** of start.
- Discounts and promotional pricing already applied to a past booking.
- Vouchers and credits as set out in § 6.

---

## 6. Vouchers and credits

Two distinct kinds of credit may appear on your account; they are treated differently on refund.

- **Paid credits** — credits you bought (for example, by pre-purchasing a bundle). These hold monetary value, are refundable pro-rata on unused balance, and do not expire for **24 months** from purchase.
- **Goodwill credits** — credits we issue at our discretion (for example, a 10% reschedule credit under the [Cancellation Policy](./cancellation-policy.md) § 4). These do not hold monetary value, are **not refundable for cash**, and expire on the date stated when issued.

Once a credit — of either kind — has been redeemed against a booking, it cannot be reinstated on cancellation of that booking except where the cancellation is caused by us (see § 2.2) or by force majeure.

---

## 7. Payments made by a third party

If a session is paid for by a parent, employer, or other third party, the right to a refund vests in the cardholder who made the original payment. The Student receiving the session is not, by virtue of being the Student, entitled to direct a refund to a different card. Refunds are always issued to the original payment method.

## 8. Chargebacks and disputes

If you believe a charge is incorrect, please contact us first at [support@mdubusistats.com] before raising a chargeback with your bank. We almost always resolve disputes faster directly. Chargebacks raised without contacting us, or raised after we have already issued a refund, may result in your account being suspended.
